About us

Guangzhou

A sampling desk in Panyu, mills in the Pearl River Delta

Ten people in one room on Qinglan Street. Partner workshops for lace, metal and buttons. The sample you approve is the piece that repeats.

Who you actually write to

Briefs land with the people in this photo, not a forwarded RFQ queue. Merchandising, sampling and packing sit on the same floor so colour, plating and stitch-on details do not travel through three offices.

  • Merchandising Reads the brief, says what we can make, and keeps the mill on the approved sample.
  • Sampling Turns a hangtag, photo or sketch into a sewable piece you can reject or lock.
  • QC & export Checks finish against that piece, then packs from Panyu with notes that travel with the carton.

Piece or yard MOQ on many SKUs

TrimGem is a one-stop garment-accessories desk in Guangzhou for apparel brands and distributors. The live pages show what we have listed first — lace, appliques, buttons, hardware. The rest of the trim pack is still quoted from a brief.

The room is small on purpose. The mill that dyes the lace is rarely the shop that plates the buckle. What we own is the path — brief, sample, QC, carton — so those details do not get lost between offices.

You bring the reference, the quantity and the calendar. We bring construction notes, a sample you can hold, and an honest date from Panyu.

EU and US programmes start with papers: mill OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 by article, REACH (including nickel on metal), ISO 9001 mill copies, and ISO / AATCC lab methods on the SKU — not a company-wide stamp. Client work lives on the cases page.

Studio

Sampling desk, mill goods and packing — as they look

We do not own every loom. We do sit with the sample, the mill lot and the carton. These are the Guangzhou desk and the goods we QC before they leave Panyu.

Compliance

Papers EU and US buyers put on the PO

A lace mill ticket does not cover a plated buckle. We name the regulation, the article and the wearer, then attach mill copies and lab reports to that SKU — not a generic “we are certified” banner.

OEKO-TEX

STANDARD 100

Mill certificates by article and product class (I–IV). Appendix 4 vs 6 called out on the quote. Lace, buttons and hardware never share one file.

REACH

EU chemicals

SVHC communication, azo dyes, lead/cadmium and nickel release (EN 1811 on skin-contact metal). US programmes add CPSIA / Prop 65 language where it applies.

ISO 9001

Mill QMS

Partner workshops with ISO 9001 systems on request. The ticket sits with the mill that dyes or plates — we put the copy in the shipment pack.

ISO tests

105 · 9227 · 17025

Colour fastness (ISO 105 / AATCC), salt spray on hardware (ISO 9227), reports from ISO 17025 labs (SGS, BV, Intertek) when the brand RSL asks.

Tickets are mill-, colour- and lot-specific. We do not print an OEKO-TEX® or ISO mark on this site as if TrimGem itself held one certificate for every trim.

Capacity

How volume actually moves

Capacity is not a slogan on a factory gate. It is sampling in Guangzhou, bulk at specialist workshops, and a carton that matches the locked sample.

Sampling

Days, not a catalogue PDF

First reply within one working day (08:00–18:00 China time). A sewable sample from a brief you can hold — then you reject it, wash it, or lock it.

Partner mills

One desk, the right workshop

Lace, embroidery, buttons and hardware do not live on one machine. We place bulk with Guangdong partners who already run that construction, then QC the lot against your sample.

MOQ & repeats

Short runs, then the same piece again

Many SKUs open from about 50 pcs or 50 yards. Repeat orders follow the approved sample — colour, plating and stitch-on details stay on that card.
