What we do

One-stop garment accessories from Guangzhou

TrimGem develops and supplies lace, embroidery appliques, sew-on buttons, scarf buckles and metal hardware for apparel brands and distributors.

Send a hangtag, a photo or a sketch. We turn it into a sample you can approve, then into bulk that matches — flexible MOQ, QC before packing, and export cartons from Panyu.

  • Lace & appliques Neckline panels, bridal flowers and corded lace for ready-to-wear and occasion.
  • Buttons & hardware Sew-on buttons, pin buckles, scarf rings and metal findings in the plating you specify.
  • Sample to carton Color, size and finish lock on the sample. Short runs and repeat orders ship from Guangzhou.

How we work

From reference to a carton you can open

One path for every trim. The sample you approve is the piece we repeat.

  1. 01 Brief Color, size, plating and end use — from a hangtag, photo or sketch.
  2. 02 Sample A sewable piece you can reject, wash or pin on a proto.
  3. 03 Confirm Lock the approved sample. Bulk follows that piece, not the photo.
  4. 04 Inspect & ship Check finish, pack, and send from Panyu on an honest date.

Send a reference. Get a sample plan.

Tell us the style, quantity and destination. We reply with what we can make, what we cannot, and when a sample can leave Guangzhou.
