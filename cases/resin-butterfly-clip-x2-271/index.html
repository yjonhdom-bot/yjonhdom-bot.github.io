Resin Butterfly Clip X2-271

Item
X2-271
Material
Zinc alloy, resin
MOQ
200 pcs
First order
200 pcs

Brief. A Bangkok OEM needed a resin butterfly clip that would hold on a linen dress strap and on hair, zinc base, 200 pcs, without the resin yellowing after a week in a shop window.

Work. X2-271, zinc alloy + resin, MOQ 200 pcs. We UV-exposed a retain, checked the clip spring, and packed 200 pcs with resin faces separated.

Result. No yellowing report after their window test. Spring still held the linen strap.
