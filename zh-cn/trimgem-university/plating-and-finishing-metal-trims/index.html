Plating, antique finishes and PVD on garment hardware

  • Buyer Guides

发布 TrimGem 于 2024年5月22日

On a zinc or brass trim, the metal you see is almost never the metal you bought. It is a finish system: strike, copper, nickel, a colour layer, maybe antique, maybe a clear lacquer. That stack is where colour, abrasion and nickel release all live.

Decorative language, technical meaning

  • Bright nickel / chrome: mirror fashion, poor at hiding fingerprints, needs a sealer on costume hardware.
  • Satin / brushed: mechanical abrasion or satin nickel; more “tailoring,” less clubwear.
  • Antique brass / antique copper / gunmetal: plate, then a darkening chemistry, then a wipe that leaves colour in the recesses. The wipe is a craft step; two operators will not match without a physical standard.
  • Spray or electrophoretic colour: used when you need a fashion pink that electroplating cannot hit.
  • Laser marking: cuts through a plate to the layer below — logos that will not rub off like ink.
  • PVD (physical vapour deposition): a vacuum coating, typically more abrasion-resistant than a cheap wet plate. Cost and fixture marks are the constraints.

Protective steps are not optional on steel: copper then nickel (then chrome or a colour) is still the classic anti-rust stack. Chrome-free passivation on some systems is a REACH/RSL topic, not a marketing sticker.

What to put on the purchase order

Name the colour standard (a sealed trim, not a phone photo), the substrate, whether antique is required, whether a clear coat is required, and the tests:

  • Neutral salt spray (ISO 9227 NSS or ASTM B117) — hours to white rust / red rust as agreed.
  • EN 1811 if the piece is skin-contact in the EU.
  • Adhesion (tape or bend, as the lab method specifies).
  • For kids’ metal: lead and cadmium content, not only colour.

A 48-hour NSS pass is a common mid-market apparel ask; 96 hours shows up on better outerwear hardware. CASS (copper-accelerated acetic salt spray) is a harsher automotive-type test and is usually overkill for a fashion sew-on — unless a brand RSL says otherwise.

If you change only the lacquer, you may change salt-spray and nickel-release results. Treat the finish as part of the article number, not as a free colourway.
